Delete your account

Last updated: 28 October 2026

You can permanently delete your Placd account and all data linked to it at any time, either in the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  • Open Placd and sign in.
  • Go to the You tab.
  • Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

  • Email placd.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
  • Use the subject line "Delete my Placd account".
  • We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, sign-in credentials)
  • Your profile (education, target company and role, links)
  • Interview answers, scores and AI feedback
  • Daily question attempts, streaks and XP
  • Resume text, resume scores, optimized versions and history
  • Feedback you sent through the app

What may be kept

Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten. We keep nothing else, unless the law requires it.
