Privacy Policy

Place Like Pro (PLP)

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Place Like Pro is an AI-powered placement preparation platform that helps students and early-career professionals practice interviews, improve resumes, and track their career readiness.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the app’s features.

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Password (encrypted authentication)

User Content

  • Resume files uploaded by you
  • Interview responses and practice sessions
  • AI feedback and progress history

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to operate the app and provide its core functionality.

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide AI mock interview practice
  • Analyze resumes and generate ATS scores
  • Generate personalized AI feedback
  • Save your interview history and progress
  • Improve the reliability and security of the service

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

Your data is processed only by trusted service providers required to deliver the app’s functionality, such as secure cloud infrastructure and AI services.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including secure authentication, encrypted network communication, and protected cloud storage.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account remains active. If you delete your account, your personal information, resumes, interview history, and associated content will be permanently deleted, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data

Account deletion instructions are available on our Account Deletion page.

7. Children's Privacy

Place Like Pro is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
