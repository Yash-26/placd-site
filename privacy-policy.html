Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Placd ("we", "us") is an interview and resume preparation app for students and early-career job seekers, developed by Lumis. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. By using Placd you agree to this policy.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Profile details: college, degree, branch, graduation year, target company and role, experience level, and optional LinkedIn and GitHub links.
  • Interview content: the answers you type, and the scores and feedback generated for them.
  • Resume content: the text extracted from resumes you upload, your resume scores, and any optimized versions. We do not store the original PDF file.
  • Feedback and support messages you send us.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in session tokens, so you stay signed in.
  • Your practice activity inside the app, such as streaks, XP and progress.
  • The app version and basic technical information included with feedback or support requests.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, call logs or SMS, and we do not use an advertising ID.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To run mock interviews and generate scores and coaching feedback.
  • To analyse, score and improve your resume, and to export it as a PDF.
  • To show your history, progress, streaks and achievements.
  • To answer your feedback and support requests.
  • To keep the app reliable and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. AI processing

To score answers and analyse resumes, the relevant content (your answer and its question, or your resume) is sent to Google's Gemini API over an encrypted connection. Google processes it to return a result to us, under the Gemini API terms. We send only what is needed for the task, never your password.

5. Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers who process data on our behalf and only to run the app:

  • Supabase: account authentication and database hosting. Your data is stored in Supabase's Mumbai (India) region.
  • Google (Gemini API): AI scoring of interview answers and resume analysis.
  • Google Play: app distribution and, where offered, payments. We never see your full card or UPI details.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal data only with the service providers above, or when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. If Placd is ever transferred to another owner, this policy will continue to apply to your data.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Delete account). This permanently removes your profile, interview history, resumes, feedback and progress from our active systems straight away. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. You can also request deletion by email; see https://yash-26.github.io/placd-site/delete-account.html.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Database access rules ensure each user can read only their own records, and server-side functions verify your identity before doing any work. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Your rights

Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update it (most of it can be edited in the app).
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.
  • Raise a grievance with us, as described below.

10. Children

Placd is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app before they take effect.

12. Contact and grievance officer

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal data, contact our grievance officer at placd.support@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 days and resolve requests within 30 days.
