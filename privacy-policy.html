Privacy Policy

Place Like Pro (PLP)
Effective Date: 31 August 2026

Place Like Pro is an AI-powered placement preparation platform designed to help students and early-career professionals prepare for interviews, improve their resumes, and track their placement progress.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the features of Place Like Pro. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Resumes uploaded for analysis
  • Interview responses and practice history
  • Progress and performance within the app

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve your experience in Place Like Pro. This includes:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Providing personalized interview practice and feedback
  • Reviewing and improving your resumes
  • Saving your interview and resume history
  • Tracking your learning progress and performance
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the app

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

Protecting your information is important to us. We use secure technologies and encrypted connections to safeguard your personal data and ensure that only you can access your account information.

4. Third-Party Services

Place Like Pro uses trusted third-party services to support essential features such as secure account authentication, cloud data storage, and AI-powered resume and interview feedback. These services process information only to provide the functionality of the application.

5. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile section of the app. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, resumes, interview history, progress data, and other associated information. This action cannot be undone.

6. Children's Privacy

Place Like Pro is intended for users aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
